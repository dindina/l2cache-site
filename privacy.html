Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

L2Cache keeps your history on your Mac. The app has no account, sends nothing to servers of ours, and contains no analytics or tracking. A few features connect to the internet, and they are listed below.

What the app stores, and where

Your history — clipboard items, screenshots and the text in them, terminal commands, Git activity and coding-agent sessions — is stored in a database on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/L2cache/. We don't operate any server that receives it, and we can't see it.

Sensitive items

Sensitive items are detected on your Mac and stored as hidden, locked entries. Detected categories include API keys (OpenAI, GitHub, Stripe, AWS, Slack and others), credit card numbers, PEM private keys and certificates, US Social Security numbers, Wi-Fi credentials, and secret or password assignments in config and environment files. Their content is never shown in plain text — unlocking requires Touch ID or your login password — and it is never sent to any of the services below.

AI processing

Titles, tags and transforms are produced on your Mac, by Apple Intelligence (on-device, where your Mac supports it), a local Ollama model that you run yourself, or built-in rules. L2Cache does not send your history to cloud AI services.

Features that connect to the internet

  • Link previews. When you copy a link, L2Cache fetches that page's title, description and icon from the website itself, as your browser would. For TikTok links it uses TikTok's public oEmbed service. The website sees a normal request from your Mac.
  • Translate. When you choose Translate on a clip, the selected text (up to 5,000 characters) is sent to Google Translate.
  • Skills catalogue. When you browse skill suggestions, L2Cache loads the public catalogue from skills.sh.
  • Coding agents. If you connect the L2Cache MCP server, your coding agent can search your clipboard history, screenshots and saved folders. If you turn on hooks, recent clipboard items and the previous session's context are added to each new agent session. The MCP server and hooks run on your Mac, but your agent sends what it reads to its own AI provider, under that provider's terms. Sensitive items are never shared. Both are off until you turn them on.
  • Links you open, such as the App Store or our support page, open in your browser as usual.

Analytics

The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs. Our website, l2cache.app, uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits without cookies. It has no access to anything in the app.

Data sharing

We don't sell or share data, and we never receive your history. When you use one of the features above, the request goes directly from your Mac to that service.

Children's privacy

L2Cache does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app is rated 4+ on the Mac App Store.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes.
