Why You Should Never Upload Unencrypted PDFs to Web Converters

Protecting documents with passwords is a standard security requirement when sending W-2s, 1099 tax returns, payroll schedules, bank statements, and legal NDAs. Standard online PDF converters upload your unencrypted document across the internet, creating an immediate security vulnerability.

The 100% In-Browser Guarantee: This tool executes cryptographic key derivation and PDF stream encryption purely inside your browser memory. Enable Airplane Mode or disconnect from Wi-Fi to verify: the encryption executes completely offline with zero network connectivity.
