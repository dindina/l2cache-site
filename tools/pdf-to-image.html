Why In-Browser PDF to Image Conversion is Safer

Converting confidential documents (invoices, pitch decks, resumes, signed contracts) into images is often required for presentations, social sharing, and documentation.

Traditional online image converters transmit your entire document across the internet to a third-party server. By rendering directly on an HTML5 Canvas inside your browser's V8 engine, your confidential files never leave your machine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert multi-page PDFs?
Yes. Every page is rendered asynchronously into an individual image. You can download each page individually or click 'Download All Images'.
Is the conversion resolution high enough for printing?
Yes. Selecting 'Ultra HD Print (300 DPI)' renders at 3x scale, providing razor-sharp typography and crisp vector line art.
