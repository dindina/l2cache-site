How Fancy QR Codes Stay 100% Scannable

Standard QR codes rely on a high-contrast matrix of dark and light modules with three prominent position-detection markers (the finder "eyes" in the top-left, top-right, and bottom-left corners). Built directly into the ISO/IEC 18004 QR specification is Reed-Solomon Error Correction.

🛡️ Reed-Solomon Recovery

With Level H error correction, up to 30% of the code area can be obscured, stylized, or replaced with a brand logo without losing a single bit of payload data.

📐 Quiet Zone Preservation

Our generator automatically maintains the required 4-module quiet zone margin, ensuring smartphone cameras on iOS and Android locate the boundary effortlessly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will custom dot shapes scan reliably with iPhone and Android cameras?

Yes. Modern mobile camera sensors analyze brightness thresholds at the center coordinates of each grid cell. As long as the foreground color maintains strong contrast against the background (at least 4.5:1), rounded dots, squircles, and classy curves scan just as fast as classic squares.

Can I print these QR codes on business cards and posters?

Absolutely. You can download vector SVG files for infinite scalability in Figma, Adobe Illustrator, or Canva. If exporting as PNG, choose 4000px Ultra-HD Print for crystal-clear 300+ DPI physical printing.

Do these QR codes ever expire?

No. These are static QR codes. The destination URL or Wi-Fi password is encoded directly into the pattern itself. There are zero redirects, zero accounts, and zero expiration dates.
