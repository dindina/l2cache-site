Why Combine Images to PDF Client-Side?

Creating a single PDF out of multiple receipts, ID cards, handwritten notes, or wireframe screenshots is a daily necessity for freelancers, developers, and designers.

Online PDF services upload your private photos across the internet. By compiling the document in client memory using WebAssembly and pdf-lib, your personal photos remain private and safe.

Does this reduce image quality?
No. JPG and PNG image streams are embedded losslessly directly into the PDF container without unwanted downsampling.
