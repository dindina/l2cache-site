Frequently Asked Questions

How does the automatic format detection work?
The engine analyzes opening characters, syntax tokens, and structural AST signatures ({}, , key: value, comma delimiters) to identify whether the input is JSON, XML, YAML, or CSV.
Can it convert nested JSON to CSV?
Yes. When an array of objects has nested properties, objects are safely flattened and serialized into stringified CSV columns.
