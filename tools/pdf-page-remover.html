The Danger of Uploading Sensitive PDFs to Web Converters

PDFs frequently contain highly confidential records: bank statements, tax filings (W-2s, 1099s), medical charts, employee contracts, and trade secrets. When you upload files to traditional online PDF tools (like Smallpdf or iLovePDF), your entire document is transmitted to a remote server, often stored in temporary buckets, and processed outside your legal jurisdiction.

How this tool works differently: This tool loads the WebAssembly binary of PDF.js and PDF-lib directly into your web browser's memory. All rendering, page extraction, and PDF compilation occurs on your local CPU. Turn off your Wi-Fi or enable Airplane Mode to verify: the tool functions with zero internet connectivity.

How to Remove Pages from a PDF in 3 Steps

  1. Select or Drag & Drop your PDF: The file is read locally in milliseconds.
  2. Click Unwanted Pages: A red ✕ and badge will mark pages scheduled for deletion. Click again to undo.
  3. Export New PDF: PDF-lib strips the selected pages and downloads a pristine, full-resolution PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a page count or file size limit?
Because processing happens in your local browser memory, files with 100+ pages are supported without artificial upload limits or subscriptions.
Are vector graphics and text layers preserved?
Yes. Unlike image-based tools that rasterize PDFs, PDF-lib copies original vector object trees, embedded fonts, and text layers without re-compression.
