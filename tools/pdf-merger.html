Why You Should Never Upload Confidential PDFs to Online Merge Tools

Merging documents is a daily task when assembling mortgage applications, tax filing packages, board decks, legal contracts, and medical histories. Standard online PDF mergers upload your full files to third-party web servers. This tool runs 100% in your browser using WebAssembly: your files never leave your computer's RAM.

How to Combine Multiple PDFs in 3 Steps

  1. Upload Your PDFs: Drag and drop all the files you want to combine.
  2. Set the Sequence: Use the ↑ and ↓ arrows to arrange files in the exact order you want them to appear.
  3. Merge & Download: Click the Merge button to produce a single consolidated PDF instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are bookmarks and hyperlinks preserved when merging?
Yes. PDF-lib copies the internal document structures, high-resolution vector assets, and fonts directly into the new container.
Is there a maximum number of files I can merge?
No. Since execution runs on your device's memory rather than a remote cloud server with artificial quotas, you can combine dozens of files simultaneously.
