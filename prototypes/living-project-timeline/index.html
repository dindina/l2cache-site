Local-first, precisely explained

Your history lives on your Mac.

Core history is stored locally. L2Cache can use on-device Apple Intelligence where available, local Ollama, or rule-based processing. Features that contact a website, coding agent, translation service, or optional cloud provider have a separate network boundary.
