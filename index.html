Common Questions

How much does L2Cache cost? +
L2Cache is a {{PRICE}} one-time purchase with lifetime access. Pay once, own forever — all future updates are included with no recurring subscriptions.
Does L2Cache keep my clipboard history private? +
Yes. L2Cache stores your clipboard history locally on your Mac and its AI runs entirely on-device using Apple Intelligence. No clipboard content is ever sent to any server.
What Macs does L2Cache work on? +
L2Cache runs on macOS 13 Ventura or later. Core clipboard history and search work on any supported Mac; the on-device AI features (smart titles, Smart Transform) require macOS 15.1+ on Apple Silicon.
How is L2Cache different from the built-in macOS clipboard? +
macOS only keeps the last copied item. L2Cache keeps unlimited history with full-text search, OCR search inside screenshots, smart albums, regex grep, JWT/JSON decoding, SQL data generation, custom scripts — and works back to macOS 13.
