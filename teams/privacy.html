Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026 · Session Intelligence for Teams is a working name for a product in development by Amvotech, the makers of L2Cache.

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist we store what you enter: your email, and optionally your name, company, team size, the operating systems and coding agents your team uses, and your comment. We also store where you came from (a referring site or campaign tag), your browser's user-agent string and the time you signed up.

We use this only to contact you about early access and to plan the product. We don't sell or share it, and we don't add you to other mailing lists.

Where it is stored

Signups are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. The form sends them through a server function hosted by Vercel; the database is not reachable from the web page.

Analytics

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits without cookies.

Deleting your details

Email support@amvo.store from the address you signed up with and we'll delete your entry.

The product itself

This page covers the waitlist only. The product is designed so that agent conversations, code and diffs stay on each developer's machine; a full privacy policy for it will be published before launch.
