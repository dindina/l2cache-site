Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. The Foundation of HingeJoy

HingeJoy is built on a simple principle: Your physical device telemetry belongs exclusively to you. We believe software should be joyful, tactile, and completely private by default.

2. Data Collection & Storage

HingeJoy operates 100% locally on your iPhone Duo:

  • Hinge Telemetry: When Fold Sounds or another hinge experience is active, hinge angle, closing direction, angular velocity, duration, and fold counts are processed in memory and stored only on your local device storage.
  • Widget Sharing: If you use the HingeJoy widget, the app shares the latest local fold summary with the widget through Apple's app group storage on the same device.
  • No Cloud Analytics: We do not track your device location, IP address, browsing behavior, or unique advertising identifiers (IDFA).
  • No User Accounts: HingeJoy does not require you to create an account, register an email, or connect social profiles to use the app.

3. Hardware Health Integrity

HingeJoy provides playful physics telemetry (angle, velocity, fold counts, shut speed). We do not claim to diagnose mechanical wear or hardware health, and we never manipulate your physical hardware beyond native Apple UIKit interaction frameworks.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app trackers, or data broker frameworks. If you choose HingeJoy Pro, the transaction is processed securely and directly by Apple. HingeJoy does not receive your payment details.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding privacy or data handling in HingeJoy, contact our team at: support@amvo.store
