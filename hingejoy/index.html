Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Which devices are supported? +

HingeJoy is built natively for Apple’s iPhone Duo, taking direct advantage of its dual-screen layout and UIKit physical hinge angle APIs.

Does HingeJoy measure mechanical "Hinge Health" or wear? +

No. We take engineering integrity seriously. HingeJoy provides playful physics telemetry (angle, velocity, fold counts, shut speed). We do not claim to measure mechanical wear, battery degradation, or hardware health unless Apple provides legitimate public APIs for it.

Is my fold data stored privately? +

100% on-device and private. We do not track your location, require accounts, or send telemetry to cloud advertising servers. Your fold counts and speed records stay securely on your iPhone.

How much will HingeJoy cost? +

HingeJoy is Free to download with core sounds and daily fold counting. The first 50 waitlist members receive a free lifetime Pro unlock — no strings attached. After that, Pro is a one-time $3.99 purchase giving you lifetime access to all sound packs, custom synthesized instruments, and extended telemetry history.
