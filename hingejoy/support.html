HingeJoy for iPhone Duo

Getting Started

HingeJoy requires an iPhone Duo running iOS 18 or later. Open the app with your device unfolded — the app is active and listening as soon as it's on screen. Fold your phone to trigger sounds and record telemetry.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • No sound on fold? Make sure your ringer switch is not set to silent. HingeJoy uses system audio and respects the silent/ringer toggle.
  • Hinge angle reads 0°? Fully open the device before launching the app. If the issue persists, force-quit and relaunch HingeJoy.
  • Telemetry history cleared? Fold history is stored locally. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, local data is not preserved unless you had iCloud backup enabled.
  • How do I unlock Pro features? Tap the Pro badge inside the app and follow the in-app purchase flow. All purchases are processed directly by Apple.

TestFlight Beta Issues

If you're on the TestFlight build and encounter a crash or unexpected behavior, please include your iOS version, device generation, and a description of what you were doing when it happened. Screenshots and screen recordings are always helpful.

Restore Purchases

To restore a previous Pro purchase on a new device, open HingeJoy, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. You must be signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
